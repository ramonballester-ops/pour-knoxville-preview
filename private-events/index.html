Host Your Event In the Old City

Birthdays, corporate happy hours, rehearsal dinners, team nights, watch parties — 68 self-pour taps make Pour the easiest great party you'll ever throw.

Why Pour Works for Groups

Everyone pours their own party

  • 68 self-pour taps — beer, wine, cider & seltzer, so every guest finds their thing
  • Reserve a section or buy out the whole taproom
  • Pay-by-the-ounce keeps bar tabs honest — or set a hosted tab with a cap
  • Patio + indoor seating in Knoxville's historic Old City
  • Food packages from our kitchen — shareables, sammiches & boards
  • Board games, music and a staff that loves a good party
  • Minutes from downtown hotels, Gay Street & Market Square

Good to know

Private events are typically booked 2+ weeks out. Dates around Vols home games go fast.

How It Works

Be your own bartender

STEP 01

Check in

Grab a pour wristband at the front — it keeps a tab of everything you pour, so there's no waiting on a bartender all night.

STEP 02

Tap & pour

Walk the wall, tap your wristband on anything that looks good, and pour your own — a two-ounce taste or a full glass. Every tap shows what's flowing, its style and ABV.

STEP 03

Pay by the ounce

You're only charged for exactly what you pour. Sample your way across Tennessee craft without committing to a single pint.

🚗 Parking & good to know

  • Street parking along Jackson Ave and throughout the Old City — free evenings & weekends.
  • Old City / Jackson Ave garages within a two-block walk.
  • Rideshare drop-off: right out front on West Jackson Ave.
  • Covered boardwalk patio + indoor seating — board games and darts inside, dogs welcome on the patio.
  • Smokies game day? The ballpark is right up Jackson Ave — pour your pregame and walk over.
